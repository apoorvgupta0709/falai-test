# Project rules

## Money and commercial decisions

Before any action or decision with commercial implications (paid generation on fal.ai, Sarvam, OpenArt and others; choosing model, resolution, duration or quality; retries or variations; paid scraping or search credits; buying credits), follow `.claude/skills/paid-action-approval/SKILL.md`. Verify the real price for the exact settings, offer a cheaper option, and get the user's explicit approval first. Never quote a base `unit_price` as the cost.

## Product video ads

For product ads, reels or shorts, follow `.claude/skills/falai-ad/SKILL.md`: seven stages, each ending with human approval.
